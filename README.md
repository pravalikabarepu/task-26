# Superstore Management Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents a one-page management dashboard built using Power BI and the Superstore dataset.

The dashboard is designed to provide a concise view of overall business performance through key performance indicators, sales and profit analysis, product performance, sales trends, and interactive filters.

## Objective

Design a concise and interactive business report that helps management quickly understand sales, profitability, order volume, regional performance, category performance, and sales trends.

## Tool Used

Power BI

## Dataset

Superstore dataset

The dataset contains information about orders, customers, products, categories, regions, sales, profit, and order dates.

## KPI Definitions

### Total Sales

Total revenue generated from all orders.

### Total Profit

Total profit earned from all orders.

### Profit Margin %

Percentage of sales retained as profit.

Formula:

Profit Margin % = Total Profit / Total Sales × 100

### Total Orders

Number of unique orders placed.

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Profit Margin %

### Visualizations

* Sales by Category
* Profit by Region
* Sales by Segment
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Top 10 Products by Sales

### Dynamic Filters

* Category
* Segment
* Region
* Order Date

These filters allow users to interact with the dashboard and analyze specific parts of the business.

## Key Insights

* Technology generates the highest sales among the three product categories.
* The West region has the highest profit compared with the other regions.
* Sales fluctuate across months, with noticeable changes over the reporting period.
* A small group of products contributes significantly to total sales.
* Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments contribute different shares of overall sales.

## Dashboard Design

The dashboard uses a dark navy header with colorful KPI cards and clean visual panels. The layout is designed to keep important business information visible on a single page while maintaining readability and easy navigation.

## Conclusion

The Superstore Management Dashboard provides an interactive one-page business report for monitoring sales, profit, orders, margins, product performance, regional performance, and sales trends. It demonstrates how Power BI can transform business data into a concise and management-friendly reporting solution.
